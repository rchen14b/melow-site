Melow Pro

Melow Pro unlocks Melow. You can have it in either of two ways, both bought through the App Store with your Apple Account:

  • Monthly. An auto-renewing subscription, billed every month at the price shown in the app before you subscribe.
  • Lifetime. A one-time purchase at the price shown in the app. It unlocks Melow Pro for as long as Melow is offered, with no further charges.

Free trial

New subscribers to the monthly plan can try Melow Pro free for one week, if the app offers it to your Apple Account. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the free week ends, the subscription begins and your Apple Account is charged the monthly price. One free trial is available per Apple Account. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription or the lifetime purchase.

Renewal and cancellation

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when a free trial ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically each month unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings: on iPhone and iPad, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on a Mac, the App Store › your name › Account Settings › Subscriptions. Melow’s Settings also lead there.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals; Melow Pro stays unlocked until the end of the period you have paid for.

Restoring and devices

Melow Pro works on every iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. To bring a purchase to another device, use Restore Purchases in Melow.
