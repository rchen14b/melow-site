Support

Questions, problems or ideas: melow_player@icloud.com. I read every message.

What you need

  • iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, or a Mac with macOS 26.
  • An Apple Account with Apple Music. Your library plays without a subscription; the Apple Music catalog, For You, Recently Played and Top Charts need one.

My shelves are empty

Melow shows the library on your device. Turn on Sync Library so your Apple Music library is there:

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings › Apps › Music › Sync Library.
  • Mac: open the Music app, then Music › Settings › General › Sync Library.

Give it a few minutes to download a large library, then reopen Melow.

A song will not play

Songs from the Apple Music catalog need an active Apple Music subscription, and some music is not available in every country. Songs in your own library play without one.

Melow Pro

How do I cancel the monthly plan?

On iPhone and iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Melow. On a Mac: App Store › your name › Account Settings › Subscriptions › Manage. You can also get there from Melow’s Settings. Melow Pro stays unlocked until the end of the period you have paid for.

I bought Melow Pro, but another device asks me to buy it again

Make sure that device is signed in to the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases on the Melow Pro screen or in Settings.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose Melow.

Is Apple Music included in Melow Pro?

No. Apple Music is a separate subscription from Apple. Melow Pro unlocks Melow itself.

Lyrics

Lyrics come from LRCLIB, a free, community-maintained database. A song may have no lyrics there yet, or lyrics without timing; you can add or correct them on LRCLIB. Lyrics can be turned off in Melow’s Settings.

Getting in touch

Email melow_player@icloud.com. It helps to include your device, its system version, and Melow’s version (shown at the bottom of Melow’s Settings).
