Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

In short: Melow has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. It does not collect, sell or share your personal data, and it does not track you.

Melow is an independent music player for iPhone, iPad and Mac. In this policy, “I” and “me” mean Melow’s developer, who is named on Melow’s App Store page. This policy explains what information Melow uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Apple Music

With your permission, Melow uses Apple’s MusicKit to:

  • show your Apple Music library, your playlists, music you played recently and Apple Music’s recommendations for you;
  • play music from your library and, with an Apple Music subscription, from the Apple Music catalog;
  • add songs to your Apple Music favorites, or remove them, when you tap the heart.

These requests travel directly between your device and Apple under your own Apple Account. I never receive them, and Melow does not copy your library or listening history anywhere. Apple’s handling of this information is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Melow asks for this permission only when you tap Connect Apple Music. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Media & Apple Music on iPhone and iPad, or System Settings › Privacy & Security › Media & Apple Music on a Mac.

Lyrics

The player can show the words of the song playing, from LRCLIB, a free, community-maintained lyrics database. To find them, Melow sends LRCLIB the song’s title, artist, album and length. It does not send your name, your Apple Account, your library, or any device or advertising identifier. As with any request on the internet, LRCLIB’s servers see the network (IP) address it comes from.

Lyrics Melow has already fetched may be kept in a cache on your device so they appear quickly next time. You can turn lyrics off in Melow’s Settings, and Melow then makes no requests to LRCLIB.

Purchases

Melow Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. Melow only checks, on your device, what your Apple Account is entitled to; it never sees your payment details. Apple’s handling of purchases is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Settings on your device

Your preferences in Melow, such as the shelf it opens on, the background and the play mode, are stored only on your device.

What Melow does not do

  • It does not collect personal data, and I run no server that could receive any.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.
  • It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • It does not sell or share information about you with anyone.

Children

Melow is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Keeping and deleting information

Melow keeps information only on your device. Deleting Melow removes its settings and caches. Your Apple Music library and favorites are kept by Apple and managed in the Music app. Since I hold no information about you, there is nothing for me to delete; if you have a question about this, write to me at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in Melow’s release notes.
